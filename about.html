About Me

Curious about how systems work and how ideas become usable tools.

Who I Am

My background includes coursework and projects involving programming, web development, software design concepts, and collaborative development tools. I enjoy learning how different technologies fit together and how small design decisions can affect the final result.

Web development is useful to me because it combines structure, design, and communication. While I do not see myself focusing only on web development professionally, building this site is a practical way to present my technical background and project work.

How I Think

I like breaking problems into smaller pieces.

Structure First

I usually start by figuring out what the problem is asking for, what the constraints are, and what parts need to connect.

Practical Iteration

I prefer building a working version first, then improving organization, readability, and edge cases as the project develops.

Clear Communication

Whether it is code, documentation, or a visual layout, I think the final product should make the underlying idea easier to understand.
